The Day It Fell Apart

By Leslie Fish

A ballad of doing what must be done when the systems break down

A disaster-response story-song: when an explosion overwhelms a small-town hospital, one administrator throws out the rulebook to save lives, and answers for it afterward.

A recording is on YouTube.
