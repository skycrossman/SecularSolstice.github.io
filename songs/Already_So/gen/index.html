Already So

By Taylor Smith

A sung setting of the Litany of Gendlin: what is true is already so

A gentle, building arrangement of the Litany of Gendlin ("What is true is already so..."), moving from "I" to "you" to "we" as the room learns to bear what is true.

A recording is on YouTube.

A chord chart is preserved in chords.txt.
