# Moment of Courage
## By Skyler Crossman

So for this solstice, with the aim of becoming stronger. . .

Take a moment. Sit here in the darkness and uncertainty.

Think of something you want to try and do next year, something that feels scary to face.

Think of the most important thing. If you had the courage to face one challenge, what is it?

Think of something that matters. If not to the world, then to you.

Actually think about the question for one minute, by the clock.

[turn out the last light, wait for sixty seconds.]
