# Know Victory
## By Skyler Crossman

*This is the callback to the one knowably-false statement planted earlier in "Welcome to Bravery" (the claim of being "100% confident"). Use Alternate 1 if no one in the audience calls out the lie; use Alternate 2 if someone does.*

## Alternate 1 (Nobody spots the lie)

[Let the pause linger]

So.

Earlier I asked you to look for the one knowably false thing I would say this solstice. It'd take a little bravery, to speak up in a crowd.

And a little later, I told you that 0 and 1 are not probabilities. That "100% confident" is just not really a thing anyone gets to know.

Just now I said I'm 100% confident some day every human would grow up in health and safety. That we'd last as long as the stars did.

And I- I am not, actually, sure of that. I am maybe 60%. I am trying to level up and become stronger and maybe someday do something to move that number up a little. But for tonight, for the moment. . . I want to be around people who will tell me if I'm falling short, if I ask for that.

I'm not sure if this ask was fair or not. It is a very weird thing to do during a holiday celebration. But then, this is a weird holiday. I really wish I could be that sure, that we were on a safe path to human flourishing for ages untold. But I don't think we are, and I don't know what moves to make to change that.

When we don't know the whole path, we can still try and do the next right thing.

## Alternate 2 (Somebody spots the lie)

What? C'mon, right now? Really, you want to stop things at this moment to try and correct something?

What's your correction?

*gap*

Well. . . you're correct. I am not that sure.

For anyone who missed it- I told you earlier that 0 and 1 are not probabilities. That "100% confident" is just not really a thing anyone gets to know. Just now I said I'm 100% confident some day every human would grow up in health and safety. That we'd last as long as the stars did. That was my one lie.

I am not, actually, sure of that. I am maybe 60%. But for tonight, for the moment. . . I am really glad to be around people who will tell me if I'm falling short, if I ask for that. Thank you [name], for having the courage to speak up for what you thought was true.

I'm not sure if this ask was fair or not. It is a very weird thing to do during a holiday celebration. But then, this is a weird holiday. I really wish I could be that sure, that we were on a safe path to human flourishing for ages untold. But I don't think we are, and I don't know what moves to make to change that.

When we don't know the whole path, we can still try and do the next right thing.
